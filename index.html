    OLIVA FIRME & ASSOCIATES LAW FIRM

    ESTABLISHED IN JUNE 2008, OLIVA FIRME & ASSOCIATES LAW FIRM FORMALIZED THE PROFESSIONAL PARTNERSHIP BETWEEN ATTY. ROMMEL V. OLIVA AND JUSTICE NESAURO H. FIRME, A COLLABORATION THAT BEGAN IN 2004.

    BUILT ON INTEGRITY, LEGAL EXCELLENCE, AND CLIENT-FOCUSED SERVICE, THE FIRM HAS GROWN INTO A TRUSTED LEGAL PARTNER FOR BUSINESSES, ORGANIZATIONS, PUBLIC FIGURES, AND INDIVIDUALS ACROSS DIVERSE INDUSTRIES.

    WITH STRONG EXPERIENCE IN CORPORATE AND COMMERCIAL LAW, LITIGATION, LABOR MATTERS, CONTRACT DRAFTING, LEGAL OPINIONS, REGULATORY COMPLIANCE, AND BOARD GOVERNANCE SUPPORT, THE FIRM DELIVERS PRACTICAL AND STRATEGIC LEGAL SOLUTIONS.

    EXCEPTIONAL LEGAL REPRESENTATION GUIDED BY PROFESSIONALISM, ETHICS, AND LASTING CLIENT PARTNERSHIPS.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

DO YOU WORK AS OUTSIDE COUNSEL TO IN-HOUSE LEGAL TEAMS?

YES. MANY OF OUR RELATIONSHIPS ARE WITH IN-HOUSE LEGAL AND RISK TEAMS THAT RELY ON LEXAXIS PARTNERS AS A STRATEGIC EXTENSION OF THEIR INTERNAL CAPABILITIES. WE TAILOR ENGAGEMENT MODELS TO YOUR TEAM’S STRENGTHS, PREFERRED WORKFLOWS, AND REPORTING STRUCTURES.

WHAT INDUSTRIES DOES OLIVA AND PARTNERS LAW FIRM FOCUS ON?

WE PRIMARILY SUPPORT TECHNOLOGY, FINTECH, HEALTHCARE, AND DATA-INTENSIVE ENTERPRISES. OUR ATTORNEYS COMBINE SECTOR FLUENCY WITH DEEP EXPERIENCE IN CORPORATE, REGULATORY, AND DISPUTES WORK, ALLOWING US TO MOVE QUICKLY IN COMPLEX OR FAST-CHANGING ENVIRONMENTS.

CAN YOU SUPPORT MATTERS ACROSS MULTIPLE JURISDICTIONS?

YES. OUR TEAM HAS LED MATTERS ACROSS NORTH AMERICA, EUROPE, AND KEY ASIA-PACIFIC MARKETS. WE COORDINATE CLOSELY WITH TRUSTED LOCAL COUNSEL WHERE NEEDED, WHILE MAINTAINING A SINGLE STRATEGIC VIEW FOR YOUR LEADERSHIP AND BOARD.

HOW DO YOU STRUCTURE FEES FOR ENTERPRISE ENGAGEMENTS?

IN ADDITION TO TRADITIONAL HOURLY ARRANGEMENTS, WE FREQUENTLY PROPOSE ALTERNATIVE FEE STRUCTURES INCLUDING SCOPED PROJECT FEES, PORTFOLIOS, AND RETAINERS ALIGNED TO YOUR BUDGET, PREDICTABILITY NEEDS, AND INTERNAL APPROVAL PROCESSES.
