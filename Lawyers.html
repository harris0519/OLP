    ABOUT THE FIRM

    OLIVA FIRME & ASSOCIATES LAW FIRM



    ESTABLISHED IN JUNE 2008, OLIVA FIRME & ASSOCIATES LAW FIRM FORMALIZED THE PROFESSIONAL PARTNERSHIP BETWEEN ATTY. ROMMEL V. OLIVA AND JUSTICE NESAURO H. FIRME, A COLLABORATION THAT BEGAN IN 2004.

    BUILT ON INTEGRITY, LEGAL EXCELLENCE, AND CLIENT-FOCUSED SERVICE, THE FIRM HAS GROWN INTO A TRUSTED LEGAL PARTNER FOR BUSINESSES, ORGANIZATIONS, PUBLIC FIGURES, AND INDIVIDUALS ACROSS DIVERSE INDUSTRIES.

    WITH STRONG EXPERIENCE IN CORPORATE AND COMMERCIAL LAW, LITIGATION, LABOR MATTERS, CONTRACT DRAFTING, LEGAL OPINIONS, REGULATORY COMPLIANCE, AND BOARD GOVERNANCE SUPPORT, THE FIRM DELIVERS PRACTICAL AND STRATEGIC LEGAL SOLUTIONS.

    EXCEPTIONAL LEGAL REPRESENTATION GUIDED BY PROFESSIONALISM, ETHICS, AND LASTING CLIENT PARTNERSHIPS.
